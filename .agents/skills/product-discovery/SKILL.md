# Skill: product-discovery

## 1. Description & Purpose
Guides product discovery, user persona definition, healthcare value proposition validation, and customer problem-space exploration for MediCompare India.

## 2. Scope & Applicability
Problem framing, customer interviews, hypothesis generation, and evidence-based healthcare consumer pain points.

## 3. When to Use
When defining product direction, validating consumer needs in medicine pricing, or scoping initial feature sets.

## 4. Required Inputs
- Target user segments (patients, chronic illness caregivers, price-sensitive consumers)
- Geographic parameters (Bengaluru focus)
- Problem statements on medicine price opacity

## 5. Execution Procedure
1. Map stakeholder personas (chronic patients, elderly caregivers, independent retail pharmacies).
2. Identify core problem assumptions (price variance, stock availability, discovery friction).
3. Formulate falsifiable hypotheses regarding medicine price disparities in target areas.
4. Assess user friction points in existing pharmacy visits and online pharmacy delays.
5. Synthesize findings into actionable product discovery briefs.

## 6. Safety Constraints & Guardrails
- Do not extrapolate single-store observations to the entire Indian market. Ground every conclusion in empirical evidence.
- Strictly respect least-privilege boundaries and privacy mandates.
- Maintain transparent data provenance at all times.

## 7. Expected Outputs & Deliverables
- Product Discovery Brief
- Validated Problem-Solution Matrix
- User Persona Profiles

## 8. Validation Checklist
- [ ] Are user pain points clearly articulated?
- [ ] Are assumptions stated explicitly as falsifiable hypotheses?
- [ ] Is the geographic scope bounded to Bengaluru initial rollout?
