# Skill: competitor-analysis

## 1. Description & Purpose
Systematically assesses competitive landscape (Tata 1mg, Pharmeasy, Apollo 24/7, Netmeds, Jan Aushadhi, local retail) against MediCompare.

## 2. Scope & Applicability
Competitor feature benchmarking, business model analysis, delivery vs discovery trade-offs, and competitive positioning.

## 3. When to Use
When refining MediCompare's competitive edge or evaluating competing digital healthcare platforms.

## 4. Required Inputs
- Competitor platform features
- Pricing transparency levels
- Delivery fulfillment models

## 5. Execution Procedure
1. Benchmark major digital platforms on key parameters: price comparison transparency, local pharmacy discovery, prescription requirements, fulfillment speed.
2. Highlight the "e-pharmacy conflict": e-pharmacies prioritize their own centralized warehouses/dark stores, alienating local brick-and-mortar retail.
3. Position MediCompare's unique differentiator: neutral comparison engine empowering local neighborhood pharmacies without delivery delays or warehouse bias.
4. Detail Jan Aushadhi public sector generic availability and pricing disparity.
5. Compile competitive positioning matrix and battlecards.

## 6. Safety Constraints & Guardrails
- Do not base competitive claims on speculation; verify features against current publicly visible application interfaces.
- Strictly respect least-privilege boundaries and privacy mandates.
- Maintain transparent data provenance at all times.

## 7. Expected Outputs & Deliverables
- Competitive Analysis Matrix
- Competitor Battlecard
- Differentiator Audit

## 8. Validation Checklist
- [ ] Are competitors assessed objectively?
- [ ] Is MediCompare's neutral, discovery-first value proposition highlighted?
- [ ] Are e-pharmacy delivery delays vs immediate local pickup contrasted?
