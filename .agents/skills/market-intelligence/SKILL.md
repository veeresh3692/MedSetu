# Skill: market-intelligence

## 1. Description & Purpose
Conducts market intelligence and industry analysis on the Indian retail pharmacy landscape, pricing dynamics, and retail supply chains.

## 2. Scope & Applicability
Indian retail pharmacy structure (organized vs unorganized), trade margins, supply chain tiers, and consumer purchasing patterns.

## 3. When to Use
When evaluating business viability, pharmacy onboarding incentives, or regional rollout strategies.

## 4. Required Inputs
- Market data sources
- Retail pharmacy industry publications
- NPPA reports

## 5. Execution Procedure
1. Analyze the dual structure of Indian pharmacy: organized chains (Apollo, MedPlus) vs independent standalone pharmacies.
2. Examine retail trade margins (typically 16% on price-controlled formulations, 20-30%+ on non-scheduled branded generics).
3. Map consumer price sensitivity and generic adoption (Jan Aushadhi vs private brands).
4. Assess independent pharmacy pain points (inventory turnover, chain competition, digital visibility).
5. Formulate strategic recommendations for market entry in Bengaluru.

## 6. Safety Constraints & Guardrails
- Distinguish between Pan-India trends and city-specific Bengaluru market realities (high digital literacy, dense independent retail network).
- Strictly respect least-privilege boundaries and privacy mandates.
- Maintain transparent data provenance at all times.

## 7. Expected Outputs & Deliverables
- Market Intelligence Dossier
- Pharmacy Ecosystem Map
- Margin Analysis Summary

## 8. Validation Checklist
- [ ] Are data sources cited with publication dates?
- [ ] Is the retail margin structure realistically represented?
- [ ] Are regional nuances factored in?
