# MEDICOMPARE INDIA — RISK REGISTER

This register tracks operational, technical, legal, compliance, and security risks identified for MediCompare India along with active mitigations and owners.

---

| Risk ID | Category | Risk Description | Likelihood | Impact | Severity | Mitigation Strategy | Owner | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RSK-01** | **Regulatory** | Misinterpretation of platform as an unlicensed online pharmacy under Drugs & Cosmetics Act 1940. | Low | Critical | **HIGH** | Explicit statutory disclaimers on every view stating MediCompare does not dispense or sell drugs; discovery and price comparison only; physical prescription required at store. | Agent 04 | **MITIGATED** |
| **RSK-02** | **Data Integrity** | Conflating NPPA government ceiling prices with live retail pharmacy selling prices, misleading consumers. | Medium | High | **HIGH** | Strict physical database separation (`government_price_references` vs `pharmacy_offers`); explicit UI labeling; automated schema checks. | Agent 05 & Agent 06 | **MITIGATED** |
| **RSK-03** | **Clinical** | Inappropriate formulation matching (e.g. matching Sustained Release with Immediate Release, or different salt strengths). | Medium | Critical | **CRITICAL** | Strict multidimensional matching algorithm requiring exact salt, exact strength, compatible form, and identical release profile (IR != SR != ER). Flag substitutions clearly. | Agent 06 | **ACTIVE** |
| **RSK-04** | **Privacy (DPDP)** | Collecting sensitive health data or patient prescription records without DPDP 2023 compliance. | Low | High | **HIGH** | Anonymous search by default (no user account required for price lookup); zero storage of patient health records; minimal telemetry. | Agent 04 & Agent 12 | **ACTIVE** |
| **RSK-05** | **Security** | Accidental exposure of Supabase `service_role` secret in frontend client bundles or public repositories. | Medium | Critical | **CRITICAL** | Git pre-commit hooks, strict `.gitignore`, automated secret scanning, and hard rule: `service_role` key used strictly in server-side Route Handlers. | Agent 12 | **ACTIVE** |
| **RSK-06** | **Operational** | Stale or unverified pharmacy inventory and pricing leading to consumer complaints at physical stores. | High | Medium | **HIGH** | Mandatory `observation_timestamp` and `verification_status` displayed prominently; "Report Incorrect Price" feature for crowd-sourced audits; pharmacy freshness score. | Agent 16 | **ACTIVE** |
| **RSK-07** | **Architecture** | Premature architectural complexity (microservices, complex message queues) exceeding solo developer capacity. | Medium | High | **HIGH** | Strict adherence to Modular Monolith architecture; ban on microservices and Kubernetes for MVP; solo developer review gate. | Agent 07 | **MITIGATED** |
| **RSK-08** | **Financial** | Runaway cloud infrastructure costs from third-party map APIs or cloud hosting tiers. | High | Medium | **MEDIUM** | Use free and open-source Leaflet + OpenStreetMap tiles instead of proprietary Google Maps; host on Vercel Hobby + Supabase Free/Pro tier with hard budget alerts. | Agent 14 | **MITIGATED** |
| **RSK-09** | **Legal / IP** | Ingestion of copyrighted or terms-restricted medicine databases without appropriate data licensing. | Medium | High | **HIGH** | Exclusively use public government datasets (NPPA Gazette orders, CDSCO public lists) and direct pharmacy catalog uploads; conduct data provenance reviews before ingestion. | Agent 05 | **ACTIVE** |
| **RSK-10** | **Quality** | Unverified or broken features slipping into production without independent review. | Medium | High | **HIGH** | Enforce 10 Quality Gates; no implementing agent may approve their own work; mandatory Product Owner Level 5 sign-off for release. | Agent 18 | **ACTIVE** |
